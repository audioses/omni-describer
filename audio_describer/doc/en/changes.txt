AI check of style: past tense, interpretation instead of description, leaving the fiction (actors, camera); shown in the editor, counted by V and in the evaluation.
Professional review: status (to review / validated) and notes per description, saved in the project.

Describe my screen: the screen showing the active window is used; live mode waits for a pause in the computer's sound before speaking.
Evaluate this audio description (E in the player): the CSA 2020 questionnaire in two phases (listening without the picture, then checking with it), each question rated 1 to 5 with comments, measured values shown next to the questions they inform, no overall score.
