Local voices (Settings > Audio Output > Woman's voice / Man's voice)

Engine: sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 (https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx), Apache License 2.0.
It bundles onnxruntime (MIT) and the espeak-ng phonemizer.

Voices are NOT shipped with Omni Describer. When the user chooses one and presses
"Download and listen", the archive is downloaded from
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/tts-models, checked against the
SHA-256 pinned in audio_describer/core/piper_catalog.py and unpacked into
%LOCALAPPDATA%\OmniDescriber\piper.

Each archive contains a Piper model (https://github.com/rhasspy/piper, MIT engine) and
espeak-ng-data (GPL-3.0 data files). Every voice keeps the licence of the recordings it
was trained on, stated in the MODEL_CARD file inside its folder; some voices are for
non-commercial use only. Check it before using a voice in a commercial production.
